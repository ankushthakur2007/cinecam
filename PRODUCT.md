# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users

- Primary users: solo filmmakers, videographers, and creators.
- Situation: mobile-first content shooting as a single operator.
- Job: quickly set and lock camera controls while actively framing and recording.

## Product Purpose

CineCam exists to give mobile creators practical cinema-style manual control without leaving the capture context.

Success means users can set and lock core parameters (ISO, shutter, white balance, and related manual controls) from one place and capture reliably on Android devices without depending on network access.

## Positioning

Unlike stock camera apps that hide or automate key controls, CineCam is manual-control-first and keeps high-value capture controls together in one place for faster operator decisions during shooting.

## Operating Context

- Native Android phone capture workflow.
- Single-operator content creation sessions.
- Real shooting environments where quick manual adjustments are required.
- Offline operation is expected as a normal mode, not a fallback mode.
